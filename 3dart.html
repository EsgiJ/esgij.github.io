Project Description:

My first realistic character. The goal was to run the entire 3D character pipeline end to end, from block-out to final render, without skipping the parts I was bad at.

Pipeline:

  • Base mesh: proportions and silhouette established first.
  • Sculpting: body and face in Blender, working from muscle structure and anatomy.
  • Hard surface: every armour piece and the Dragonslayer modeled separately.
  • Retopology: clean low-poly mesh suitable for animation.
  • Rigging and weight painting: full range of motion.
  • Texturing: skin, armour and weapon in Substance Painter.
  • Rendering: Cycles.
